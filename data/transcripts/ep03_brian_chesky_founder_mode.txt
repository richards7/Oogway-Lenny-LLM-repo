Lenny: Today on Lenny's Podcast, we have Brian Chesky, co-founder and CEO of Airbnb. Brian, welcome!

Brian Chesky: Hey Lenny, glad to be here.

Lenny: Brian, your talk at Y Combinator about 'Founder Mode' went viral and sparked a massive debate across Silicon Valley. What does Founder Mode actually mean in practice?

Brian Chesky: Founder Mode is the alternative to traditional 'Manager Mode'. For decades, business schools taught founders that as a company grows, you should hire executive leaders, delegate everything to them, and just oversee them through high-level review meetings. But in practice, when founders step away from the details, companies lose their soul, product quality degrades, and political bureaucracy takes over.
Founder Mode means leaders stay deeply involved in the details of product design, customer experience, and cross-functional execution. It’s not micromanagement—it’s hands-on leadership where executive leaders run the company alongside the founder in a unified roadmap.

Lenny: How did you restructure Airbnb’s product and marketing organization to implement this philosophy?

Brian Chesky: We abolished the traditional decentralized product management structure where 50 different product managers were running 50 independent A/B tests. Instead, we consolidated the whole company onto ONE centralized annual product roadmap. We combined Product Management and Product Marketing into a single function called Product Marketing Managers (PMMs). Now, the people defining what we build are also responsible for how we position and launch it to the world.

Lenny: What is your approach to design and craft at Airbnb?

Brian Chesky: Design is not just how something looks; it is how it works end-to-end. At Airbnb, we don't build software through spreadsheets or Jira tickets alone. We design the entire user journey through storyboards and interactive prototypes. Every single feature must serve a clear story in the guest or host journey. If a detail feels unpolished to a customer, it is a defect, period.

Lenny: What advice do you have for tech founders trying to scale without losing momentum?

Brian Chesky: Don't hire managers who just manage people; hire leaders who can do the work and craft the product themselves. Keep your team small, keep your roadmap focused, and never delegate the core product vision.
